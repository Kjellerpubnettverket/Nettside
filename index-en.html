About the Network

The Basement Pub Network is a collaboration between the student-run basement pubs at the University of Oslo. We work to create a vibrant and inclusive social environment on campus.

Our member organizations run basement pubs that are more than just places where you can buy affordable coffee and food – they are meeting places where students build friendships, exchange ideas, and find belonging. Each pub is run by volunteer students who are passionate about creating great experiences.
